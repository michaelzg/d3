About this experiment +

These fictional population totals are anchored to real city locations. Seeded clusters are scattered around each center and clipped to land. Each panel count has a composed arrangement of portrait and landscape maps, with intentional space between them. On each page load, one to three randomly selected regions receive the largest slots and a soft gold spotlight. That selection stays visible as the count changes. Population subtly scales the remaining panels within their slots; area is not a quantitative population encoding. The hero stays at 2:1 on larger screens and 4:5 on phones, regardless of count. Map projections refit to each panel. Every map uses its own scale, so dot concentration should not be compared as measured population density. Resimulate generates a new set of populations and clusters.
